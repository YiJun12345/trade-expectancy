How to use & assumptions

1R is the initial risk on your whole position. A 1.5R target on a 25% allocation earns 0.375R.

Costs are average entry and exit fees, net funding and slippage divided by initial monetary risk. The default 0.05R is illustrative. Avoid double-counting costs already in your returns.

Excluding TP3 removes its profit credit, but keeps its initial downside. Required TP2 assumes unchanged hit rates; farther targets may be less likely to fill. No trailing stops or early discretionary exits are modelled.

Expected profit is a long-run average, not a prediction for this trade or a position-sizing recommendation.
